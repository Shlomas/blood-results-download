A clearer timeline

From a PDF to a useful view

Blood Results organizes report values so you can compare dates and focus on the part of a trend that matters to you.

  1. 1

    Import a report

    Select a blood-test PDF. The app matches a layout profile and reconstructs its results table.

  2. 2

    Review every result

    Confirm names, dates, values, units and reference ranges. Create or clone a mapping profile when the layout is unfamiliar.

  3. 3

    Explore the trend

    Choose tests, dates and a normalized focus range in one view.

Private by default

Your reports stay with you

The desktop app works without an online account. Medical content and original PDFs are stored in password-protected, encrypted profiles on your computer. The app does not include analytics or cloud synchronization.

  • Separate profiles for people sharing one computer
  • Password-encrypted portable backups
  • Automatic locking after inactivity and Windows lock

Keep your password and a backup safe. There is no server-side copy and no password recovery.
